# Privacy Policy for LEO AI

**Last Updated:** October 24, 2026  
**Effective Date:** October 24, 2026

At **LEO AI** ("we", "our", or "us"), accessible through our Android application, your privacy and data security are our top priorities. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, retention, security, and disclosure when you interact with our application, services, and associated platforms.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use LEO AI, we may collect and process the following categories of information:

### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
- **Conversation Transcripts:** Chat messages, session titles, and timestamps created while interacting with LEO AI are stored locally on your device within our sandboxed Room Database (`leo_ai_room.db`).
- **User Preferences:** Local preferences such as theme settings, input drafts, and history groupings.

### B. Account Information (If You Register an Account)
- **Identity & Contact Details:** Email address, username, profile identifiers, and authentication credentials.
- **Account Metadata:** Account registration date, last active session, and preferences.

### C. Financial & Transaction Data (If You Make Purchases or Subscriptions)
- **Payment Processing:** When you purchase subscriptions, in-app features, or credits, transactions are processed by third-party payment gateways (e.g., Google Play Billing, Stripe, PayPal). We do not directly store complete credit card numbers or banking credentials on our servers.
- **Transaction Records:** Order IDs, purchase timestamps, subscription statuses, and purchase history.

### D. Device and Technical Information
- **Telemetry & Diagnostics:** Device model, operating system version, app version, language settings, and crash logs.
- **Identifiers:** Pseudonymous device or advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID) where permitted by system settings.

## 3. Data Retention and Right to be Forgotten (GDPR & CCPA)

We adhere to the principle of data minimization and clear data retention standards:

### A. Data Retention Schedules
- **Local Device Transcripts:** All conversation transcripts and session metadata stored locally in the application's Room Database remain on your device until you proactively delete them or uninstall the application.
- **Server and Diagnostic Logs:** Any ephemeral server-side diagnostic, network, or performance logs are retained for a maximum of thirty (30) days and automatically purged thereafter.
- **Account Information:** Account records and subscription entitlements are retained for as long as your account remains active and in good standing.

### B. User-Initiated Local Deletion
You maintain full control over conversation history stored on your device:
- **Instant Local Wipe:** Navigate to **Settings > Clear all conversations** inside LEO AI to permanently delete all stored chat sessions, messages, and previews from your local database immediately.
- **Individual Session Deletion:** In the **Conversations History** screen, tap the delete icon on any individual session to erase that conversation and its associated messages.

### C. Right to be Forgotten (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)
- You have the legal right to request the complete deletion of your account and any associated personal data held on our servers ("Right to be Forgotten" / "Right to Erasure").
- To exercise this right, send an email to **creatergame31@gmail.com** with the subject line *"Data Deletion Request"*.
- Verified deletion requests are executed across all active production databases and backup archives within thirty (30) calendar days.

## 4. Data Security Measures

We implement multi-layered administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your information:
- **Industry-Standard Encryption:** All network communications with external endpoints or servers use industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2+ / HTTPS). Sensitive credentials and tokens are protected using standard AES encryption.
- **Password Protection:** User passwords are never stored in plaintext; industry-standard cryptographic hashing and salting are applied.
- **On-Device Sandboxing:** Local databases are stored strictly within the application's protected private internal storage sandbox, inaccessible to other standard Android applications.

## 5. Third-Party Sharing and Disclosures

We do not sell your personal data. We disclose information only under the following circumstances:

### A. Payment Processors
When processing digital goods or subscriptions, payment data is securely handled by certified third-party processors (such as Google Play In-App Billing or Stripe). These processors adhere to the strict requirements of the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI-DSS).

### B. Advertising Networks & Tracking SDKs
If third-party advertising networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads) are integrated:
- These networks may collect pseudonymous identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID), IP addresses, and interaction metrics to display relevant ads and measure ad performance.
- You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings (**Settings > Google > Ads > Delete Advertising ID**).

### C. Artificial Intelligence & Language Model Providers
When utilizing server-side large language models (such as Google Gemini via Firebase / AI Studio), conversational prompts are transmitted securely via API to generate responses. Prompts are processed strictly in accordance with enterprise data protection standards and are not used to publicly expose personal information.

### D. Legal & Compliance Requests
We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or governmental order, or when necessary to protect the life, safety, or legal rights of our users or the public.
